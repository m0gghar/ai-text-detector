Capstone Viva & Interview Preparation Guide

1. Why start with a baseline model?

A classical baseline (TF-IDF + Stylometry + Linear SVM) establishes an empirical lower bound. It proves whether the immense computational overhead and memory requirements of a deep transformer (RoBERTa) yield statistically meaningful performance gains.

2. Why RoBERTa over BERT or GPT?

RoBERTa (Robustly Optimized BERT Approach) removes BERT's Next Sentence Prediction (NSP) objective, trains with dynamic masking on 10x larger corpora, and utilizes bidirectional context. Unlike autoregressive decoder models (e.g. GPT), bidirectional encoders capture holistic syntactic cohesion across the entire passage.

3. What does TF-IDF capture, and what does it miss?

TF-IDF captures lexical frequency anomalies and n-gram repetitions (such as formulaic transition markers common in LLMs). However, it is an unordered bag-of-words representation that completely ignores syntactic word order, long-range dependencies, and semantic polysemy.

4. Why use F1-score and ROC-AUC rather than raw Accuracy?

Raw accuracy is susceptible to class prevalence skew. In educational forensics, false positives (wrongly accusing an honest student of using AI) carry catastrophic academic consequences. F1-score balances precision and recall, while ROC-AUC evaluates discrimination capacity across all threshold settings.

5. What are the key failure modes and limitations?

Detectors fail significantly on short texts (< 50 words) due to sparse stylistic signal, on heavily paraphrased AI outputs, and on non-native English speakers whose formal vocabulary often triggers false positives.
