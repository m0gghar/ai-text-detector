## 2. Contamination & Deduplication Methodology

- **Standard Wording**: *No exact or near-duplicate matches found at the 0.80 Jaccard threshold (MinHash is probabilistic).*
- **Filtering Protocol**:
  1. Exact match SHA-256 hash against all 341,049 Kaggle training texts.
  2. Normalized 200-character prefix hash.
  3. `datasketch` MinHash LSH (128 permutations, 5-gram shingles, Jaccard threshold $\ge 0.80$).
- **Uniform Length Threshold**:
  - $W \ge 50$ words applied across all main benchmarks. Texts with $< 50$ words are segregated into `short_text_analysis`.

## 3. Paraphrased AI Dataset Construction Methodology

Because the paraphrased set is an evaluation stress test, its generation protocol is formally documented:
1. **Source Texts**: Generated by ChatGPT (GPT-3.5) on domain prompts.
2. **Paraphrase Transformation**: Lexical synonym substitution targeting 25%–35% of content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives) and voice alterations (active to passive / clausal re-ordering).
3. **Validation**: Manually inspected to verify semantic preservation, grammatical fluency, and retention of original argument structure.

## 4. Kaggle Internal Train-Test Overlap Audit Findings

Evaluated using MinHash LSH ($N=128$, character 5-grams, Jaccard threshold $\ge 0.80$) querying 3,000 held-out test texts against a 15,000-text training partition:
- **Test Texts Evaluated**: 3,000
- **Training Texts Indexed**: 15,000
- **Near-Duplicates Detected**: **551 (18.37%)**
- **Clean Texts (No Match at 0.80 Jaccard)**: 2,449 (81.63%)
- **Accuracy on Texts WITH Near-Duplicate in Train**: **100.00%** (1.0000)
- **Accuracy on Clean Texts (WITHOUT Near-Duplicate)**: **99.96%** (0.9996)
- **Scientific Conclusion**: While near-duplicate prompt overlap accounts for 18.37% of the test partition, the model's high in-domain score is primarily driven by global corpus-level stylistic segregation (formal ChatGPT vocabulary vs. informal high-school student grammar), rather than isolated text memorization alone.
